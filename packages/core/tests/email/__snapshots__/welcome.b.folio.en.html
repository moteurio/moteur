What is Moteur?

Moteur is where teams write, translate and publish their websites’ content. Every change is kept, and the content stays theirs.

To start

1.Start your first site’s project, in its languages.
2.Invite your team: each with their role, and nothing they can break.
3.Connect the site: a channel, a key that only reads, and the content flows.
